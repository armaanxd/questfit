QuestFit Privacy Policy

What we read

With your permission, QuestFit reads three kinds of data from Health Connect: steps, workouts (exercise sessions) and heart rate. It never writes to Health Connect.

Why

To verify your quests, for example confirming that a walk or workout really happened.

Where it goes

This data stays on your device. It is never sent anywhere and never sold.

Revoking access

Open Health Connect, go to App permissions, choose QuestFit and remove its permissions or delete its data. You can still complete quests with the timer.
